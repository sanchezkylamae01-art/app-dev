# Kyla's Favorite Movie

## Pride & Prejudice
- **Story by:** Jane Austen
- **Director:** Joe Wright
- **Genre:** Romance, Comedy, Drama
- **Release Date:** July 25, 2005

### Summary
In 18th-century England, the witty Elizabeth Bennet and the arrogant Mr. Darcy must overcome their mutual pride and prejudice—fueled by class differences and miscommunications—to finally realize and accept their deep love for one another.
